      Replay results

      Gross = change in contract value. Net assumes closing all positions at each observation, with both entry and exit fees and slippage. Taxes, exchange charges, financing and margin are excluded. Maximum drawdown is in rupees on the observed net path, not a return percentage or a win rate.
